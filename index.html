Sticker printer settings

Sticker sheet

Matches your current sticker layout. Printing to the label printer works once this is built in Bolt.

Departments

The coloured department buttons on the till, in this order. Type a price then tap one to ring it up. A department can't be removed while stickers use it.

Button groups

Each group is a button on the till (like "Mixes & mulch ›") that opens its own set of buttons. Sales from a group's buttons are reported under the group's name at end of day.

Buttons

The preset buttons inside each group. Leave the price blank and the till asks for one when you tap it. Fill in "Deal qty" and "Deal price" for multi-buys, e.g. 2 for $22. Buying that many or more gives every one the deal price (3 = $33).

Receipts and cash drawer

The cash drawer is plugged into the receipt printer and opens whenever something prints. OD with no sale prints a small "No sale" slip to open it.

Behind the scenes: sticker codes

The till keeps this list itself. Each sticker barcode stands for one department at one price. You can fix a sticker's name here; scans show the new name straight away, but stickers already printed keep the old wording.

Quotes & invoices

Prices include GST. Invoices are due in 7 days and quotes last 30 days, unless you change them. Tap a row to open it.
